Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Summary

Pedalya needs no account and uses no ads or usage analytics; only if the app crashes, a technical error report is sent. Your location is not sent to our servers.

Location

Your location is processed on your phone to show nearby places and distances. Apple's address service may be used to identify your country or city. You can turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time.

Stored on your phone

Your settings, saved places and country packs stay only on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

Data packs

When you download or update a pack, the request goes to GitHub. GitHub may technically process your IP address.

Reporting wrong info

Reports are sent with your email app. If you send one, we see your email address and message and use them only to correct the data and reply to you.

Other services

The map is shown with Apple Maps. If you start directions, Apple Maps or Google Maps opens; those apps follow their own privacy policies.

Data source

Place and cycle path data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the ODbL licence.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a technical report with the error message, app and iOS version and device model is sent to Sentry. The report does not include your location, name or email address. Sentry may technically process the IP address the request comes from.
